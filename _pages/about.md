I work on consensus for blockchain protocols, revisiting classical problems such as state machine replication from fresh perspectives and designing new foundational computational models (see publications below for examples). My long-term goal is to advance both the theoretical foundations and practical applications of distributed systems, and I am always open to working on exciting new projects in this space.
